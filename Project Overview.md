# Walmart Sales Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview

The **Walmart Sales Performance Dashboard** is an interactive Business Intelligence solution developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze and monitor weekly sales performance across stores and time periods.

The dashboard converts raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive KPIs, trend analysis, store-level comparisons, monthly performance analysis, and holiday sales evaluation.

The primary goal of this project is to provide a centralized and interactive view of sales performance that can support **data-driven decision-making, performance monitoring, and business analysis**.

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze Walmart's sales data and answer key business questions such as:

* How are sales performing over time?
* Which stores generate the highest sales?
* How does weekly sales performance vary across stores?
* Which months show stronger or weaker sales performance?
* How do holiday and non-holiday periods compare?
* Are there noticeable sales peaks or fluctuations?
* Which areas of the business may require further investigation?

## Dashboard Highlights

### Sales Performance Analysis

* Monitors overall sales performance through KPI cards.
* Analyzes weekly sales trends across different time periods.
* Identifies major sales peaks and fluctuations.

### Store Performance Analysis

* Compares total sales across individual stores.
* Highlights high-performing and lower-performing stores.
* Analyzes average weekly sales by store.

### Time-Based Analysis

* Tracks sales performance by year, month, and day.
* Identifies monthly sales patterns and seasonal variations.
* Helps understand changes in demand over time.

### Holiday Analysis

* Compares sales performance between holiday and non-holiday periods.
* Evaluates the contribution of holiday periods to overall sales.
* Helps identify potential seasonal sales opportunities.

---

## Key Business Insights

* Sales performance shows **significant variation across different weeks and time periods**.
* A small group of stores contributes a **substantial portion of total sales**, making store-level performance analysis important.
* Weekly sales demonstrate noticeable **peaks and fluctuations**, which may indicate changes in customer demand or seasonal behavior.
* Monthly analysis reveals **variations in sales performance throughout the year**.
* Store-level comparison makes it possible to identify **top-performing stores and potential underperforming locations**.
* Non-holiday periods account for the **majority of total sales**, while holiday periods represent a smaller proportion.
* Interactive filtering allows users to investigate performance for **specific stores, dates, and holiday categories**.
* The dashboard provides a strong foundation for further analysis such as **sales forecasting, store benchmarking, and seasonal planning**.
